Asian-fusion brunch menu

Explore some of the dishes and drinks people come to SPILL THE BEANS CAFE for in Linden Park, near Burnside. A mix of familiar brunch favourites and bold Asian flavours.

Asian-fusion favourites

  • KFC Waffle

    Korean-style karaage fried chicken, a waffle and our house gochujang glaze.

  • Soba Drop

    Avocado, edamame, cucumber, pickled onions, carrot, nori, seaweed, fried shallots and Sriracha mayo, with a choice of grilled chicken or smoked salmon.

  • Nanban CK Sando

    Fried chicken, Nanban sauce, tartar sauce, cucumber, red onion and mixed greens.

  • Chilli Scrambled Eggs

    A brunch favourite with a little extra heat.

  • Karaage Kick

    Karaage chicken with our signature Sriracha mayo, served with a mixed salad.

Brunch classics & lighter plates

  • Hash Browns Stack

    Three hash browns, double bacon, avocado, tomato salsa, a sunny-side-up egg and gremolata crumbs.

  • Haloumi Bun

    A brioche bun with three pieces of pan-fried haloumi, roasted Swiss brown mushrooms, tomato, aioli, beetroot relish and mixed greens.

  • Eggs Benedict

    A classic brunch choice. Ask about available bacon or salmon versions.

  • Avocado on Toast

    A familiar breakfast favourite with avocado on toast.

  • Mushroom Toast

    Two poached eggs and sautéed Swiss brown mushrooms with cream, served with local artichoke dip on rye sourdough toast and topped with parmesan cheese.

  • Rainbow Salad

    A colourful, fresh option for lunch.

  • Berry Waffle

    A sweet waffle option for brunch.

Coffee, matcha & drinks
